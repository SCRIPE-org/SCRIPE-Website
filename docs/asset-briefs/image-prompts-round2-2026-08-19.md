## 1. ⚠️ MUST REDO — Day midground cutout (the delivered one has a defect)

*Why: the delivered "Golden-Hour Sports Complex Diorama" was shot at a closer, tilt-shift/miniature angle that does not match its background plate — the parallax layers would not line up — and it has a red fringe along the stadium roofline.*

> The stadium and swimming pool isolated on a fully transparent background (alpha PNG), shot as a **wide, gently-tilted aerial photograph** — drone at ~120m altitude, 25° downward tilt, 24mm full-frame lens — matching a golden-hour morning aerial establishing shot of the same campus exactly. Explicitly NOT tilt-shift, NOT a miniature/diorama look: normal depth of field throughout, no selective blur, no exaggerated perspective convergence. Golden morning light, long soft shadows, pool water turquoise, turf natural green. Soft feathered edges, absolutely no color fringing or halo at the alpha boundary. 21:9 canvas, transparent PNG.

## 2. Day finale — the "living map" at morning (missing; blocks the full day hero)

> Directly overhead (nadir, 90° top-down, ~200m altitude) view of a multi-sport campus at mid-morning, composed like a living map: stadium bowl, Olympic pool, football pitches, tennis and padel courts reading as clean geometric shapes. Natural daylight color — green turf, turquoise pool, pale court surfaces — with long soft shadows giving the map relief. The main walkway reads as a pale painted stripe. Perfectly rectilinear, subtle vignette. 21:9. Mood: many branches, one picture, in daylight.

## 3. Day foreground bokeh (missing; night version can stand in, but this is better)

> Foreground parallax layer on a fully transparent background (alpha PNG): the top of an unlit floodlight mast and dark tree canopy entering only from the bottom corners, occupying only the lowest 18% of the frame, heavily out of focus (85mm f/1.8 bokeh). Daytime — no floodlight glow; warm backlit morning rim-light along the foliage edges. The center 70% of the canvas is completely transparent and empty. 21:9 canvas, transparent PNG.

## 4. Multi-sport organizations page hero (missing)

> The widest campus frame: high-altitude night aerial at ~350m showing a full multi-sport estate as distinct pools of light separated by dark ground — floodlit stadium, glowing pool complex, cluster of lit courts, training pitches — each zone its own light temperature (amber stadium, turquoise pool, cool-white courts). 21:9. Mood: many disciplines, one estate.
