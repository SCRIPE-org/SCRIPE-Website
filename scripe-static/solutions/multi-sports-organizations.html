02Focus

What multi-sports organizations actually run on.

Centralized operations, combined KPIs, branch management, permissions and group reporting.

Multiple branches

Each branch runs its own timetable, staff and pricing — without duplicating a single member record.

Multiple sports

Programs, surfaces and bookings are sport-agnostic: padel, swimming, basketball, tennis and gymnastics beside football.

Centralized operations

One definition of a session, a member and a payment, applied everywhere.

Combined KPIs

Group revenue, membership, attendance and utilization on one line, not five exports.

Branch management

Open, pause or restructure a branch without rebuilding the organization around it.

Permissions

Roles scoped per branch and per module, so people see the operation they are responsible for.

Reporting

Board-level reporting built from the record rather than re-keyed into a deck.

Business intelligence

Peak hours, unsold capacity and coach load compared across the whole group.
