02Focus

What sports academies actually run on.

Programs, groups and sessions with attendance, subscriptions and guardian communication attached.

Development programs

Programs, groups and sessions structured by stage, not improvised each term.

Athletes

One record per athlete, carried across groups, sports and seasons.

Parents

Guardians receive session, attendance and payment updates without a phone call.

Coaches

Session plans and rosters on the phone; observations reach selection without retyping.

Attendance

Marked at the session, offline-capable, and closed the same day it happened.

Payments

Communication

Performance

Attendance, progression and trial evidence read together across the development pathway.
